@damm620 The `OUTPUT_URL=null` will not upload the ISO to TSM I believe.
However, when you do not want an ISO how will you ever recover? I guess
you want the ISO to be saved on TSM, right? And, not have a local copy
of the ISO?  
Then remove the `OUTPUT_URL=null` line and add for example:

    POST_BACKUP_SCRIPT=("rm -f  /var/lib/rear/output/rear-$HOSTNAME.iso")

but then you will need to run `rear -v mkbackup`
